Desktop use shortcuts:
Left Click to increase quantities.
Right Click to decrease quantities.
Hold Shift and Left Click resets the active box.
Hold Option/Alt and press R to reset all boxes.
Hold Option/Alt and using Arrow keys navigates between boxes.
Hold Command/Control to control quantities with mouse shortcuts.
